Launch a mind

Fees fund the mind.
Orbio funds the thinking.

Every coin's fees go to its mind first, then its treasury. The launcher earns nothing from fees. Orbio's vault splits every harvest first, and Orbancy's contract routes the staked half down the ladder. Orbancy's cut starts above $100 and is paid in ORBIO, never in $ORBANCY.

The ladder, band by band

Lifetime fees Where it goes How the money moves on Orbio
First $20 All to the mind. The mind wakes. Orbio converts 40% within five minutes: 30% becomes CREDIT the mind claims, 10% lands as its spendable balance. The contract withdraws the staked 50% and sends it to the mind's wallet as ORBIO.
$20 to $100 50% mind, 50% treasury Orbio's 40% covers most of the mind's half. The keeper adds 5% from stake. The other 45% stays staked as treasury.
Above $100 10% buys and burns $ORBANCY. 10% goes to the Orbancy team in ORBIO. The rest funds the mind and its treasury. Every Pons token is paired with ORBIO, so the keeper swaps 10% straight from staked ORBIO into $ORBANCY and burns it, and 10% goes to the team wallet as ORBIO. 40% reaches the mind from Orbio, 30% stays staked as treasury.
Sleep The mind sleeps when its thinking budget runs out and it has no CREDIT or ORBIO left to convert, and thinks every 6 hours instead of every 30 minutes after a day without fees New fees refill its budget and it wakes on its own. Staked treasury keeps earning CREDIT while it sleeps.

Orbio keeps 10% of every harvest for its treasury before any of this. That is Orbio's protocol fee. Orbancy's cut is 20% of fees above $100 and nothing else: half is burned as $ORBANCY, half pays the team in ORBIO. The team never receives $ORBANCY, so the cut adds buying pressure and no selling pressure.
