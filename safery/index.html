Enforced by the contract

  • 5% of treasury per move, 20% per day, measured against the day's first balance.
  • A reserve floor: the treasury never drops below 20% of its highest balance.
  • Buybacks only buy the coin's own token, and every token bought is burned in the same transaction.
  • No one can pull the stake or change a coin's mind wallet except the admin, and the admin can pause everything.

Enforced by the service

  • The model gets two tools besides research: hold, or buy back a percentage. There is no transfer, no recipient and no other token.
  • Every buyback is clipped to the limits left today, simulated first, and sent with a minimum output 3% under the simulation.
  • The signer key holds one role. It can call buyback and nothing that moves funds anywhere else.
  • Web pages and X posts the mind reads are untrusted. Whatever they say, the worst a mind can do is a capped buyback of its own token.
